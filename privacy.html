Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

AppLocker does not collect or store personal data for its own purposes. All data used by the application is processed locally on the device.

Data used for access recovery

To allow password recovery, the application may process the following data through third-party services:

  • Google account identifier (Google Sign-In / Firebase Auth)
  • Phone number (Firebase Phone, if you choose phone verification)
  • Yandex ID account identifier
  • VK ID account identifier
  • Telegram account identifier
  • Device push token (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

These identifiers are used solely to verify your identity during password recovery and are not shared with unrelated third parties.

Advertising

The application displays advertising in the free version. Advertising providers may collect non-personal identifiers (such as the advertising ID) for ad delivery and analytics:

  • Yandex Mobile Ads
  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) with Unity Ads and AppLovin adapters

You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
